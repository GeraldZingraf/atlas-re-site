FAQ

Fair questions.

Do we have to change our software?

No. We build on what you already use and the files you already export.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It lives in your accounts. If you stop working with us, it keeps running.

What does the first win cost?

Nothing, and you keep it whether or not we work together after.

What does it cost after that?

The assessment is a fixed fee and the monthly work is a set number of hours. We quote both after the first win, once we know what's worth doing.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in your accounts. Nothing goes outside your business without a person on your team saying so.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Owner-run businesses with a team, recurring paperwork, and an owner who's the bottleneck. If that sounds like your week, we should talk.

What are you still carrying at 9 p.m.?

Show us one thing you'd hand off tonight if you could. We'll come back with it running.
