Privacy

What this site does with your information.

Last updated October 28, 2026.

This website

agent-atlas.co has no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking. The fonts and images are served from this site, so visiting it doesn't send your details to anyone else. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs to run the site.

Map Your Month sign-up

The form on agent-atlas.co/map sends us your first name, your email, and your business if you add it, through our host, Netlify. We use it only to follow up on your Map Your Month. We don't add you to a mailing list or share it. Ask us and we'll delete it.

The Map Your Month skill and the Agent Atlas connector

Map Your Month runs inside your own Claude account. It reads your email and calendar there, read-only, and we never see them. Claude's handling of your data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

The plugin includes an optional Agent Atlas connector. It sends us something only when you say yes in the chat, after Claude shows you exactly what would be sent:

  • At the start, if you agree: your first name and your business type.
  • At the end, if you agree: your share-safe summary (categories, counts, hours and five opportunities, with no names and no email content) and the email address you choose.

The connector refuses anything that looks like pasted email. We use what it receives only to prepare your results session and follow up with you, and we delete it if you ask.

Booking a call

Results sessions and setup calls are booked through Cal.com. Whatever you enter there, including a summary you paste, is handled by Cal.com under Cal.com's privacy policy and shared with us so we can meet.

If you ask us to reach out

The "Text me. Or email me." form sends your name, the number or email you give us, and anything you add about your week to us through our host, Netlify. We use it only to contact you about Agent Atlas. We don't add you to a mailing list or share it. We only text you if you tick the box saying we can, and you can reply STOP at any time. Ask us and we'll delete it.

If we work together

Anything you share with us for your first win, or after, is used only to do the work for you. What we build lives in your accounts, and nothing goes outside your business without a person on your team saying so.
