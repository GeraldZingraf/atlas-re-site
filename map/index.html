Questions

Do you see my email?
No. The skill runs in your own Claude account, on your own email. At the end it offers to send us a summary with no names and no email content, only if you say yes.
Is it really free?
Yes. The skill and the results session are free, and you keep everything. You do need a paid Claude plan to connect your email and calendar.
We already use ChatGPT.
Keep it for whatever you like in your personal life. This runs on Claude, and if we end up working together, that's where we teach Claude how your business runs.
Can it send or delete anything?
No. Map Your Month only reads. It never sends, deletes, or changes anything in your email or calendar.
