Map Your Month · Setup

You're in. Here's the 20-minute setup.

Do this on a computer. It all happens in your own Claude account, and Map Your Month only reads: it never sends, deletes, or changes anything.

  1. Get Claude 3 min

    Sign in or sign up at claude.ai. Connecting your email and calendar needs a paid plan, Pro or higher. Already use ChatGPT? Keep it for your personal life. This runs on Claude.

  2. Turn off training 1 min

    In Claude, open Settings > Privacy and turn off Help improve Claude, so your chats aren't used to train models.

  3. Connect your email and calendar 3 min

    Go to claude.ai/customize/connectors.

    • Google: connect Gmail and Google Calendar, and allow access when Google asks.
    • Outlook: connect Microsoft 365. If Microsoft says an administrator must approve it, whoever manages your company's Microsoft 365 approves it once.

  4. Add the Agent Atlas plugin 2 min

    Go to claude.ai/customize/plugins, choose Add > Add marketplace, and paste:

    GeraldZingraf/agent-atlas-plugins

    Then add the Agent Atlas plugin from the list. On its Connectors tab you can also connect agent-atlas. That's optional: it lets Claude send us your summary in one step, and only when you say yes.

    If "Add marketplace" isn't there, download the plugin and use Add > Upload plugin instead.

  5. Say "map my month" 10 min

    Start a new chat. Make sure your email and calendar are switched on (the + button, then Connectors). Type:

    map my month

    Answer three quick questions. Claude reads your last 30 days and writes your report.

  6. Share your summary and book 2 min

    Your report ends with a short summary that has no names and no email content. Send it to us (Claude can do it, with your yes) or paste it into the booking form, then pick a time for your free 30-minute results session. We come with your first win already planned.
